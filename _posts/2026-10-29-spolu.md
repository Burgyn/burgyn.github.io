If you've ever gone on a trip with a few other families, you know the last evening.
A pile of receipts, a calculator, and the eternal question: *does a five-year-old count
as a whole person when we split the groceries?*

Apps like Splitwise split money between people. But on a family trip it isn't people
who settle up, it's families. And families aren't the same size. So I built
**[Spolu](https://spolu.top)** (Slovak for *together*).

## What it does

- **Splits between families, not people.** Each family enters its adults and kids.
  Food is split with kids at half by default, accommodation per head, ski school only
  for the kids, wine only for the adults - or any ratio you agree on.
- **Everyone writes their own receipts.** You invite the other families with a link and
  each of them logs what they paid. Everybody sees the balance live.
- **One link at the end.** Spolu works out the smallest number of transfers and gives
  you a payment QR code for your banking app (or a PayMe link). The settlement opens
  without an account, so grandma can see it too.

- **Not just money.** Each event also has a shared plan - deposits with reminders,
  who brings the grill - and a chat for all the families.
- **Works without signal.** It's a web app you add to your home screen. At a cottage
  with no Wi-Fi you keep writing and it syncs once you're back online.

It speaks Slovak, Czech, Polish and English and works with euros, korunas, złoty or
any other currency. It's free.

## Try it

If you're planning a ski week, a summer cottage or just a long weekend with friends'
families, give it a go at **[spolu.top](https://spolu.top)**. And if something
annoys you or is missing, let me know - I'd love the feedback.
